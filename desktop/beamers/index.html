Beamers

1. Flash the LazyTO firmware

Reading the firmware…

  1. Unplug the beamer.
  2. Hold its button while you plug it into this laptop, then let go.
  3. Click Flash.

The beamer keeps its station number and the replays it holds. Flashing takes about a minute; keep it plugged in until it says done.

2. Its Wi-Fi and LazyTO's secret

Plug the beamer in normally (no button) and wait for its drive to appear. LazyTO writes the network, LAZYTO = true and this relay's secret into its CONFIG/config.txt. The rest of the file stays as it is.
